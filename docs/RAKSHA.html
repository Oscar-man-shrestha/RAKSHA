1. What the node does

RAKSHA watches heat, process-air risk, and falls. It warns the worker with a vibration motor and an RGB lamp, because a buzzer is lost in kiln and welding noise. A fan relay and a beacon relay act on the room. Exposure time is stored against the worker who checked in with an RFID card, and it counts only while that worker is in the zone.

Three decisions carry the design:

  • Presence gate. PIR or IR must see a person before heat or air time accumulates. An empty bench does not invent hours.
  • Free-fall gate. A fall is a short free-fall, then an impact, then a still posture. Shaking the bench never produces the free-fall, so kiln vibration is rejected.
  • Relays fail off. Both relays are off at boot and off on a sensor fault. Past a real threshold the fan closes. On a latched fall the beacon closes.
This kit has no gas sensor. The air result is a process-risk score: sustained shop noise, humidity that is not clearing, and heat, gated by presence. Say that in the pitch. The KY-037 is not a ppm meter.

The 10 ms fall path never waits on the DHT22, the filesystem, or HTTP. Every threshold and pin lives in one config.h.

3. Simulation on Logicraft Studios

The simulation runs in the browser at https://logicraftstudios.tech/editor/. It is the Velxio editor. It compiles the ESP32 sketch and runs it against the wired canvas. The project is a Wokwi zip, which that editor opens directly.

Open the project

  1. Open https://logicraftstudios.tech/editor/ .
  2. If a “Start a new project” panel is showing, press Cancel.
  3. Press the open-folder control, Open project (.vlx Logicraft Studios or .zip Wokwi).
  4. Choose simulation/raksha-logicraft.zip.
  5. Press Libraries and confirm these four are installed: DHT sensor library for ESPx, Adafruit GFX Library, Adafruit SSD1306, Adafruit BusIO. Install any that are missing.
  6. Press the compile / run control. Open Serial at 115200.
  7. In the serial send box, type RUN and send it.

The scripted run lasts about 20 seconds. It uses the same heat-index function, the same risk weights, and the same fall state machine as the bench firmware. The serial monitor is the simulation record for the pitch.

Canvas stand-ins. Logicraft has no RC522 and no KY-037. The green CARD button is the RFID tap. The left potentiometer is the sound envelope. The buzzer is the vibration motor on GPIO 16. The barometer, the IR sensor, the buck converter, and the 74HC14 stay on the bench; they are not on this canvas. The heat vote in the simulator is DHT22 against the NTC only.

Scripted scenario

Type RUN. The firmware holds the threshold at 57 and plays eight steps. Reference values below come from the same formulas as the sketch. The serial line is the result to screenshot.

RUN applies the settled 10-second sound envelope so the pitch fits in one pass. In live mode the envelope really is a 10-second average: a clap dies away, and the sound potentiometer has to be held up. Live mode also reads the DHT22 slider, the NTC slider, the PIR “Simulate Motion” button, both potentiometers, and the CARD button. Other serial commands: CARD, DROP, SHAKE, UNPLUG, HELP.

4. Algorithms

Heat index

Convert DHT temperature to °F. If the simple screening value averages under 80 °F with the air temperature, use that screening value. Otherwise use the Rothfusz regression, including the low-humidity and high-humidity corrections, then convert back to °C.

Heat index °CBandWhile a worker is present
below 27NoneGreen. Counters idle.
27 to 32CautionAmber. One short vibration pulse. Exposure seconds increment.
32 to 39Extreme cautionAmber. Exposure seconds increment.
39 to 51DangerRed. Repeating vibration. Fan on. Danger seconds increment.
above 51ExtremeSame as danger.

Temperature vote

Compare the DHT22 with the NTC and with the barometer temperature. Heat is invalid only when the DHT misses both of the others by more than 8 °C. In the simulator there is no barometer, so the comparison is DHT against NTC. Invalid heat forces the relays off and the OLED shows a sensor fault. A single disagreeing sensor does not do that.

Process-risk score

stagnation = clamp((RH − 65) / 20, 0, 1) heat_couple = clamp((heat_index_C − 27) / 12, 0, 1) risk = 100 × (0.50 × sound_ema + 0.30 × stagnation + 0.20 × heat_couple) threshold = 30 + (potentiometer × 60) range 30 to 90 air_alert = worker present AND heat valid AND risk ≥ threshold

sound_ema is a 10-second exponential average of the KY-037 analog pin, normalised to 0–1. The weights are 0.50, 0.30, and 0.20.

Fall machine

Magnitude is the root-sum-square of the three accelerometer axes in g. Mount the board with +Z up. Tilt is computed only while the magnitude is between 0.7 g and 1.3 g, so the impact spike itself cannot look like a posture change.

FromToRule
IdleFree-fallMagnitude stays under 0.4 g for at least 80 ms.
Free-fallImpactMagnitude exceeds 2.5 g within 500 ms. Otherwise return to idle.
ImpactConfirmTilt passes 60° from upright and the PIR is quiet.
ConfirmAlarmThat posture holds for 2 s. If the worker moves, return to idle.
AlarmIdleThe same worker’s card is tapped again, or a fault clears the node.

The alarm latches the beacon and the vibration. Gyro data is not used.

5. Bench hardware

Set the HW-411 to 5.0 V with a multimeter before the ESP32 is connected. That rail feeds VIN and the relay board. The vibration motor is powered from 5 V through the 2N2222A, with the 1N4007 across the motor, cathode at 5 V. The ESP32 3.3 V pin does not feed the motor or the relays.

Power the 74HC14 from 3.3 V and use it to clean the PIR and the KY-037 digital pin. A 5 V high into an ESP32 GPIO damages the pin. Analog signals stay on ADC1, because Wi-Fi uses ADC2.

7. Pitch order

The round asks for seven sections, then a defence of about three minutes.

  1. Introduction. RAKSHA, a shop node for kiln, welding, tailoring, and construction work.
  2. Problem. Heat, fumes, and falls, with no instrument and no per-worker record.
  3. Approach. Walk the pipeline. Say the presence gate, the free-fall gate, and the relays that fail off.
  4. Simulation. The Logicraft serial capture from RUN.
  5. Results. The four bench measurements, then the one-minute hardware demo if the board is built.
  6. Edge cases. Shake, empty room, unknown card, Wi-Fi loss, DHT removed. Offer the shake and the DHT pull to the judges.
  7. SDG 8. The worker gets a warning they can feel in a loud shop. The employer gets minutes of exposure tied to a person, and the fan is the first corrective action.
